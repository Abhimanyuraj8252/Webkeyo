# Privacy Policy — Webkeyo

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Webkeyo ("the app") is made by Abhimanyu Raj (Trikrypta). This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: **almost everything stays on your phone.**

## What the app stores on your device

- **Your projects** (images, PDFs, scripts, rendered videos) — stored in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any Webkeyo server, because there is no Webkeyo server.
- **Your API keys** (Sarvam, Gemini, Deepgram, Fish Audio, MiniMax, Google Cloud, Amazon Polly, Speechify, Azure, ElevenLabs, and others) — stored only on your device (Hive / SharedPreferences). They are sent only to the provider you chose, for the request you asked for. They are never sent to us, and never shared with anyone else.
- **Usage counters** (how many videos you exported, your creation streak, achievements) — stored on your device only, to show your streak and level in the app.

## What leaves your device

- **When you render a video with a cloud TTS or Vision AI provider**, the text, images or documents you chose are sent to that provider (for example Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Speechify or Sarvam) so it can generate audio or analysis. Each provider has its own privacy policy — check it in Settings before you paste a key. The app itself does not add any tracking to those requests.
- **Crash reports (optional):** if you enable Sentry in the app settings, anonymous crash reports (stack trace, device model, app version) may be sent to Sentry so bugs can be fixed. No API keys, project content or personal data is included. Sentry is off unless a DSN is configured — the app works fully without it.
- **Nothing else.** There is no account, no sign-in, no ads SDK, no analytics SDK that phones home by default, and no selling of data — there is no data to sell.

## Permissions

- **Storage / photos:** to pick manga, manhwa or PDF pages and to save rendered videos.
- **Notifications (optional):** only for the daily creation reminder you turn on yourself, and for render progress. No marketing notifications.
- **Microphone / camera:** not used.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13. If you are under 13, use it with a parent or guardian.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shipped in the app and the "Last updated" date above will change.
